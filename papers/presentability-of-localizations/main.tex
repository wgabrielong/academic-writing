\begin{abstract}
     \small{\textsc{Abstract.} We prove that certain categories of Bousfield localizations are presentable and give explicit regular cardinals $\kappa$ for which they are $\kappa$-presentable. As an application, we show that the category of analytic rings in the sense of Clausen and Scholze is $(2^{\aleph_0})^+$-presentable.}
\end{abstract}
